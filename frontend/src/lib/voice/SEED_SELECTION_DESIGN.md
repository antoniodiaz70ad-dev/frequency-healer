# Seed Selection V1

`seed-registry-v1` is exactly: 80, 112, 144, 192, 224, 256, 320, 448 and 640 Hz. The values cover low through upper-middle positions, retain 144 for compatibility, allow one octave in either direction, and support every current ratio/cascade within 40–2000 Hz. Historical popularity never enters selection.

Eligibility is a hard full-`buildSchedule` gate. Valid candidates rank by greatest minimum logarithmic boundary headroom, then lowest logarithmic center offset. The structural top tier admits candidates within 0.10 octave of the best boundary headroom and, where evaluated, within 0.10 octave of its center offset. This is a V1 product tolerance, not a scientific threshold.

N<5 never influences ordering. N=5–9 is displayed as preliminary only. N>=10 descriptive PERSONAL_N1 evidence may reorder only inside the structural tier and only between candidates that both have descriptive, exactly comparable evidence. Comparable Discovery evidence applies only afterward and under the same gate. Stable registry order is final. Duration, folklore, HCI, therapeutic claims and popularity never enter ranking.
